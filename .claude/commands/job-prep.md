# Interview prep

For: $ARGUMENTS

1. Find the job on the board (`jobs`, match company/role/id). Read its `description`,
   `fit`, `materials`, `emails`, and `profile/stories`, `profile/resume`.
2. Research (≤ 6 web searches): what the firm does, recent deals / news (last 6 months,
   with dates and sources), the team or fund if named, interview format reports for this
   firm and level.
3. Write `job.prep`:
   - `summary`: 5 bullets — the firm, the team, why the role exists, 2 recent facts (with dates).
   - `likelyQuestions`: 10–12 — motivation (why this firm / role / city), CV walk-through
     prompts, 3–4 technical questions typical for the role (e.g. restructuring: liquidity
     analysis, creditor waterfall, 13-week cash flow; PE/VC: deal discussion, valuation
     methods; consulting: a case prompt), and 2 behavioural.
   - `storiesToUse`: for the behavioural and motivation questions, which `profile/stories`
     item to tell (by id) and the one-line angle.
   - `askThem`: 4 sharp questions to ask the interviewer.
   - `logistics`: date/time (in the owner's time zone), format, who, what to bring — from the invite.
4. Never invent experience. If a likely question has no good story, say so in the prep
   ("No direct example — use the closest: …").
5. Set `nextStep` to the interview and `nextDate`. In the reply: when it is, and the 3 things
   to review first.
